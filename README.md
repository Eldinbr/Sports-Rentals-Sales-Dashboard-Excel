Project Title: **Sports-Rentals-Sales-Dashboard**

An interactive Water Sports Rentals Sales Dashboard built in Tableau Public. This project analyzes rental transactions, revenue, customer trends, equipment performance, seasonal demand, and sales metrics to uncover valuable business insights. The dashboard highlights key metrics such as total revenue, rental bookings, top-performing equipment, customer demographics, seasonal sales trends, and profitability.

## Overview

- **Motivation:** Managing water sports rental businesses requires continuous monitoring of bookings, revenue, customer demand, and equipment utilization. This project was created to transform raw rental and sales data into an interactive Tableau dashboard that enables business owners and analysts to monitor performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

- **Objective:** The primary objective of this dashboard is to analyze water sports rental sales data, identify business trends, evaluate equipment performance, monitor customer behavior, and provide actionable insights for improving operational efficiency and profitability.

- **Learning Outcomes:** While building this project, I learned:

  Tableau Dashboard Design and Storytelling, 
  Data Cleaning and Preprocessing, 
  Sales Performance Analysis, 
  Customer Trend Analysis, 
  KPI Dashboard Development, 
  Interactive Data Visualization, 
  Business Intelligence Reporting

## Dataset

Dataset Information

- Dataset Type: Water Sports Rentals Sales Dataset
- Records: Multiple rental transactions
- Features Included: 
  Rental Date, 
  Customer Name, 
  Equipment Type, 
  Rental Category, 
  Rental Duration, 
  Sales Revenue, 
  Quantity, 
  Customer Type, 
  Location, 
  Payment Method, 
  Profit, 
  Discount, 
  Region
  
- Dataset Preparation:

The dataset was cleaned and transformed before visualization:

Removed duplicate records, 
Removed null values, 
Standardized date formats, 
Cleaned customer information, 
Standardized equipment categories, 
Calculated sales KPIs, 
Created revenue and profit metrics, 
Prepared time-series data for trend analysis
  
- Dataset Background: The dataset represents rental transactions from a water sports rental business. It contains operational and financial information that enables the analysis of rental performance, customer preferences, equipment utilization, and overall business profitability.


- Important Notes:
  Sales values are based on completed rental transactions, 
  Revenue and profit calculations are aggregated for reporting purposes, 
  Dashboard filters allow users to explore multiple business dimensions interactively.

## Analysis & Visualizations 

Key Insights

- Revenue trends highlight seasonal demand for water sports activities.
- Certain equipment categories generate significantly higher rental income.
- Peak rental activity occurs during holiday and summer seasons.
- Returning customers contribute a substantial portion of total revenue.
- Profitability varies across equipment categories and rental durations.
- Regional analysis identifies the highest-performing business locations.

Dashboard Components

- Revenue Overview: Displays total sales revenue, rental bookings, and key business KPIs.

- Monthly Sales Trend: Visualizes revenue growth and seasonal demand across different months.

- Equipment Performance: Shows the highest-performing rental equipment based on revenue and bookings.

- Customer Analysis: Analyzes customer segments, repeat customers, and purchasing behavior.

- Regional Performance: Compares sales performance across different business locations.

- Profit Analysis: Displays profit contribution by equipment category and rental type.

- Payment Analysis: Illustrates customer payment method preferences.

Business Value

- Monitor business performance in real time
- Improve inventory planning
- Identify high-performing equipment
- Optimize pricing strategies
- Increase operational efficiency
- Support strategic business decisions
- Understand customer behavior
- Maximize profitability
